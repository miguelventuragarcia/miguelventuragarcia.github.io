Greetings dear visitor! Welcome to my page. 

![](images/homepage.jpeg)

If you happened to fall into this website by accident and have no idea who I am and what I do, let me introduce myself.

My name is Miguel Garcia and, from a very young age, I have always shown an intense curiosity as to *why* things work. After a rather brief situationship with the realm of biology, I ended up where I believe I am happiest: studying the beautiful abstract nonsense that is the incredible world of Mathematics.

Following a BSc in Mathematics at NOVA FCT, I wanted to go further in the study of Pure Mathematics, which led me to also obtain a MSc in Mathematics and Applications at the same institution, where I defended my thesis so called "Microlocal Equivalence of Quasi-Ordinary Surfaces". 

Given that the third time is a charm, I went even deeper and got myself enrolled in the PhD program of Mathematics and Applications at my dearest *alma mater*, where I am currently a first-year student.

My particular topics of interest that underline my research are Local Analytic Geometry and Algebraic Geometry.

Ideally, and if the Father Time allows it, I would like to write and publish some articles on this webpage that helps distill what my research actually is, and to give any curious individual an idea of what I do on my day-to-day life.

Hope you have a wonderful stay :)
