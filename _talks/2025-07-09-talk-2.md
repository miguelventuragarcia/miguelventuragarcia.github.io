For germs of plane curves, Zariski proved that an irreducible plane curve germ is analytically equivalent to an irreducible plane curve germ with finite Puiseux parametrization. Germs of plane curve germs represent the simplest instance of quasi-ordinary hypersurfaces in dimension two. Zariski’s algebraic techniques cannot be applied to the general case. We show that, for some types of quasi-ordinary surfaces, these techniques can indeed be applied in the context of contact geometry.
